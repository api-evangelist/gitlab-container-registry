Enforcements such as NIS2 are no longer a future planning consideration. The European Union Agency for Cybersecurity's (ENISA) NIS360 report confirms that supervisory authorities are actively assessing cybersecurity maturity across critical sectors. The agency is moving from guidance and consultation into active oversight, scrutiny, and accountability.
