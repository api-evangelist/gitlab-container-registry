There’s no single best model for every software development task. Implementing a new feature, diagnosing a failed pipeline, and resolving security vulnerabilities all place different demands on the model handling them. GitLab Duo Agent Platform is expanding GitLab-managed model choice with three hosted open weight models: Kimi K3, GLM 5.3, and MiniMax M3.
