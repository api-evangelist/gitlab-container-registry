There's a particular kind of frustration that happens when prompting an AI assistant with the same correction multiple times in a single session. The marvels of modern large language models (LLMs) make it so you're working with the most enthusiastic apprentice you'll ever have. However, that apprentice also happens to be an amnesiac.
